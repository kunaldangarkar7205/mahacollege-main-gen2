CAP Registration

Candidates seeking first-year B.E./B.Tech admission through the Maharashtra State CET Cell CAP process must complete the online application/registration, enter the required academic and candidature details, upload the required documents, and complete the applicable document verification/scrutiny process.

  • Use the official CAP portal for registration and candidate login.
  • Keep MHT-CET/JEE details, marksheets, category documents and other required certificates ready.
  • Check the official notice for the applicable registration, confirmation and scrutiny dates.
  • After registration, keep your Application ID and login details safe.

Provisional & Final Merit List

After applications are processed and verified, the CET Cell publishes merit-list information. Candidates should check their merit status on the official engineering CAP portal and follow the published grievance/correction instructions when applicable.

1. Provisional Merit ListCheck your provisional merit status and verify the details.
2. Grievance / CorrectionFollow the official process if any eligible correction or objection window is announced.
3. Final Merit ListThe final merit list is published after the applicable processing of grievances.

CAP Round Allotment

Candidates participate in CAP rounds by submitting the applicable option form/preferences. Seat allotment is published round-wise. The official engineering CAP portal provides allotment-status pages, cut-off lists and institute-wise allotment information.

CAP Round IOption form → allotment → seat acceptance/reporting as per the official schedule.
CAP Round IIEligible candidates can participate according to the published rules and schedule.
CAP Round IIICheck the round-specific allotment, cut-offs and seat-acceptance instructions.
CAP Round IVCheck final-round allotment, cut-offs and subsequent vacancy/ACAP notices.

What to Check After Allotment

When an allotment is published, carefully check the official instructions for that round before taking any action.

  • Allotted institute and course/branch
  • Category and seat type shown in the allotment
  • Round number and allotment status
  • Seat acceptance / self-verification instructions
  • Fee-payment and institute-reporting requirements, if applicable
  • Next-round eligibility and option-form instructions, if applicable
